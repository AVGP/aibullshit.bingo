## Challenge

Decades of valuable experimental data and clinical documentation were stored across incompatible systems, forcing research teams to spend weeks searching for historical findings.

## Solution

**AI Business Future Solutions** built a secure, unified research knowledge platform:

1. **Holistic Research Data Lake:** Indexed 25 years of laboratory and clinical records into a governed semantic repository.
2. **Generative Research Assistant:** Deployed traceable generative AI tools tailored to the company's core scientific competencies.

## Impact

Over 1,800 researchers gained instant access to verified institutional knowledge, cutting pre-clinical synthesis time by **42%** while maintaining full regulatory traceability.
