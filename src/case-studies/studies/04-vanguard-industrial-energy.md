## Challenge

Aging infrastructure and rising electricity demand required a shift from reactive repairs to predictive asset management across a trans-continental utility network.

## Solution

**AI Business Future Solutions** modernized the utility's data and analytics architecture:

* **Unified Sensor Data Lake:** Connected 120,000+ field sensors with historical maintenance logs and weather feeds.
* **Predictive Maintenance Models:** Identified equipment anomalies early and automatically prioritized field-team dispatch.

## Impact

Unplanned outages fell by **38%**, generating **$185M in annual maintenance savings** and strengthening long-term grid reliability.
