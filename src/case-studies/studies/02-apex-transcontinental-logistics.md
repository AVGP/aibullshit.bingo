## Challenge

With over 4,500 retail stores, distribution centers, and digital channels, our client faced delays between customer demand shifts and upstream inventory replenishment.

## Solution

**AI Business Future Solutions** implemented an integrated omnichannel intelligence layer:

* **Unified Touchpoint Data:** Connected POS, e-commerce, and logistics feeds into a central data lake infrastructure.
* **Predictive Demand Synthesis:** Applied machine learning models to forecast regional demand and automate inventory routing.
* **Agile Team Workflows:** Equipped merchandising and logistics teams with clear, actionable recommendations.

## Impact

The transformation improved fulfillment velocity by **28%**, reduced excess inventory holding costs by **32%**, and delivered a **3.1x bottom-line ROI** within 18 months.
