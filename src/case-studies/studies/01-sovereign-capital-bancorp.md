## Challenge

Operating across 40+ countries, this global financial institution struggled with fragmented risk and liquidity reporting across 12 separate regional data platforms. Teams spent days reconciling reports manually, slowing down executive decision-making.

## Solution

**AI Business Future Solutions** partnered with the bank's executive leadership to design and deploy a unified **holistic data lake infrastructure**:

1. **Data Consolidation:** Unified 12 regional warehouses into a single, governed data platform with real-time lineage tracking.
2. **Generative AI Analytics:** Deployed domain-tuned analytical models aligned with the bank's core risk-assessment competencies.
3. **Executive KPI Dashboards:** Delivered real-time visibility across treasury, commercial banking, and compliance teams.

## Impact

Within 12 months, the institution reduced reporting latency by **64%**, achieved **100% audit compliance**, and unlocked **$420M in annualized operational and capital efficiency**.
