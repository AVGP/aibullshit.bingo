## What is going on here?!

Okay, you might be wondering: "What the _*fuck*_ is this site?". That's a fair question.

Well, it's an experiment and to be very frank: *It's pure AI slop*.
But wait, there is more. _Some_ stuff on this page is real. It's actual content from actual consulting companies. They put it online like that. I didn't make it up, I just made sure it isn't overly self-referencing (yeah, tell me about it) and mixed it in with the rest. Scarily, it's not obvious which bits are and which bits aren't.

At least I hope you find that scary. If not: Yeah, no, I too love the revolution of synergies brought upon the enterprise ecosystem by frontier generative AI systems. (Picture me nodding reassuringly)

## Anyway - how did this happen? Why is this here?

Number one: I see far too many people being far too excited about flooding the internet with AI slop.
I don't want it, I don't think anyone wants it, yet there are people pumping it out like it's a gold rush.

> But Martin, you might say, you've now done exactly that. - A concerned smartass
Yeah, but to point out that this is bullshit. I mean, look at it, that's in the fucking domain name.

Number two: I wanted to see if people can tell which parts of this website are real (and have been taken from very dead-serious big business corporations) and what's just AI slop. I'm being fair and state the sources where such sources exists, so it's trivial if you scroll to the end of a page, but ask yourself: Without this, would you have spotted the difference?

Number three: It's fun, the domain was available, it made me laugh. Maybe you laughed, too. Have fun, don't take yourself - or this - too serious, life's too short for that shit.

So yeah, of these for text excerpts, I took two from real, actual, look-at-me-I-am-big-business articles and two from shit AI made up. The difference is marginal - at best. I [wrote about that a bit](https://thatsaweb.site/posts/the-convincing-machine/) on my blog and it struck me: Slop is slop. Even _if_ this is _human-generated_ slop, it's still slop. 

A lot of the corp speak and business-bananas we're all laughing about (I _sincerely hope_ even the dudes-in-suits and guys-in-ties do, once they leave the office, but I'm less sure every day) is effectively slop.

## Slop, you say?

Well, that's a relatively new term but the underlying concept has been around. It's formulaic - phrases, habits.
This can show up as [public speeches](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dJtHv9qKCoc) or [presentations](https://deckary.com/blog/pillar-consulting-presentations-guide).

These aren't inherently bad or a problem, but if the framework becomes more substantial than what's put into it, that's a problem.

Hilariously, that's where Generative AI shines the most, tho. It has seen it before, it has _tons_ of examples of the framework and just enough information to make it _seem_ like it has substance. And the framework supports it - we see the landmarks, we hear the key phrases, see the keywords, observe the key gestures. But it's not _real_.
